# Support & FAQ

Welcome to DraftSpace Support! Find answers to common questions below, or contact us directly.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: How do I create my first beer log?**  
A: Open the app, tap the "+" button, and fill in the beer details (brewery, style, ABV, location, rating, and notes). You can also add photos!

**Q: Can I edit a beer log after I create it?**  
A: Yes! Tap on any beer in your collection and select "Edit" to update the details.

**Q: How do I delete a beer from my collection?**  
A: Swipe left on the beer entry (or long-press) and select "Delete." You can also delete from the beer details screen.

---

### Features & Functionality

**Q: What does ABV mean?**  
A: ABV stands for Alcohol By Volume — the percentage of alcohol in the beer.

**Q: Can I rate beers with more than 5 stars?**  
A: The app uses a 5-star system. You can also add detailed notes about what you liked or didn't like!

**Q: How do I search for a beer I've logged?**  
A: Use the search bar at the top and filter by brewery name, beer style, rating, location, or date.

**Q: Can I group my beers by brewery or style?**  
A: Yes! Use the "Sort" or "Filter" options to organize your collection by brewery, style, rating, or location.

**Q: Can I attach multiple photos to a beer log?**  
A: Yes! You can add multiple photos when creating or editing a beer log.

---

### Data & Backup

**Q: How do I backup my beer collection?**  
A: DraftSpace offers local backup and cloud storage options in Settings. Choose your preferred backup method.

**Q: What happens if I delete the app? Will I lose my data?**  
A: If you have enabled cloud backup, your data is safe and will sync when you reinstall the app. If you only use local storage, your data may be lost if the app is uninstalled.

**Q: Can I export my beer logs?**  
A: Yes! Go to Settings > Export to download your data as a CSV or JSON file.

**Q: Can I import beers from another app?**  
A: We're working on import features. For now, you can manually add beers or contact support for assistance.

---

### Account & Privacy

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and follow the email instructions.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and data (deletion may take up to 30 days to process completely).

**Q: Is my data private?**  
A: Yes! Your beer logs are private and stored securely. We do not share your data with third parties. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.

**Q: How is my data encrypted?**  
A: All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols. At-rest encryption is available with cloud backup.

### Technical Issues

**Q: The app is crashing. What should I do?**  
A: 
1. Try restarting the app
2. Check that you have the latest version (update from your app store)
3. Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > DraftSpace > Storage > Clear Cache)
4. Reinstall the app if issues persist
5. Contact support with details about when the crash occurs

**Q: Photos aren't uploading. How do I fix this?**  
A: 
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try uploading a smaller photo
3. Ensure the app has permission to access your photos (Settings > Permissions)
4. Restart the app and try again
5. Contact support if the issue continues

**Q: The app is running slowly. How can I speed it up?**  
A: 
1. Close other apps running in the background
2. Clear the app cache
3. Reduce the number of photos attached to each beer
4. Update to the latest app version
5. Restart your device

**Q: I'm getting an error message. What does it mean?**  
A: Please screenshot the error and send it to support@draftspace.app. Include details about what you were doing when the error occurred.

### Suggestions & Feedback

**Q: How do I report a bug?**  
A: Email us at support@draftspace.app with:
- A description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce it
- Your device and app version
- A screenshot (if helpful)

**Q: Can I request a new feature?**  
A: Absolutely! We'd love to hear your ideas. Email support@draftspace.app or use the feedback option in the app.
