DraftSpace Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Overview

DraftSpace ("the App") is a personal beer tracking application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

DraftSpace does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter—beer names, breweries, ratings, tasting notes, and photos—is stored exclusively on your device.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally in an SQLite database on your device. Photos you attach are saved to local app storage. No data is sent to external servers.

Backups

The App provides a backup feature that creates a local zip file on your device. If you choose to upload this file to a cloud service (Google Drive, OneDrive, etc.), that transfer is handled by the respective cloud app and governed by its own privacy policy.

Analytics & Tracking

DraftSpace does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. No usage data is collected.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

Permissions

The App may request access to your device's camera and photo library solely for attaching photos to beer entries. These photos remain on your device.

Children's Privacy

DraftSpace is not directed at children under the age of 18. The App is designed for adults of legal drinking age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store listing.
